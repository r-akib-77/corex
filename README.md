## 🏟️ Overview

**CoreX Arena & Academy** is a sports-focused website concept designed to give players, teams, and aspiring athletes a clear starting point for discovering a sports facility and its training opportunities.

The website is intended to communicate the facility's identity, make key information easy to find, and guide visitors toward actions such as exploring the venue, learning about academy offerings, or enquiring about a booking.

**Live demo:** https://corex-623.pages.dev/

## 🎯 Project Goals

The project focuses on making a sports facility's online presence more useful and accessible.

- **Make discovery easy:** help visitors understand what the facility offers.
- **Promote participation:** give players and teams a clear path toward booking or making an enquiry.
- **Showcase the academy:** communicate training opportunities and the value of structured practice.
- **Build trust:** present information in a consistent, professional, and easy-to-scan layout.
- **Support mobile visitors:** make essential information and actions convenient to access on smaller screens.

## 🗓️ Booking Journey

A well-structured sports website should make the intended booking process easy to understand.

1. **Discover** — visit the website and learn about the facility.
2. **Explore** — review venue details, sports options, and academy information.
3. **Choose** — identify the facility or service that matches the visitor's needs.
4. **Enquire or book** — continue through the booking interface or contact route provided by the website.
5. **Confirm** — follow the facility's actual confirmation process.

This describes the intended customer journey; it does not imply that live slot availability, automated reservations, or payment processing are connected.

## 🎨 Design Principles

- **Action-oriented layout:** make key actions easy to identify.
- **Strong visual hierarchy:** organize content into clear, scannable sections.
- **Sports-focused identity:** use imagery and messaging that support an energetic, athletic brand.
- **Simple navigation:** reduce friction when moving between facility and academy information.
- **Mobile-conscious interaction:** keep buttons, forms, and navigation usable on smaller screens.
- **Performance-aware implementation:** avoid unnecessary assets, scripts, and animation overhead.
- **Consistent presentation:** maintain coherent typography, spacing, and component behavior throughout the site.
